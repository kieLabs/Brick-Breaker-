HOW TO PLAY

1. DRAG TO AIM, RELEASE TO SHOOT: Touch & drag anywhere on the screen to aim the beaded neon laser sight (`• • • •`). Release your finger to fire a rapid stream of glowing balls!
2. FLOOR LANDING & BALL GLIDE-BACK: Just like Play Store Bricks Breaker Glow, the first ball to hit the bottom floor sets your next launch position! Collect green (+1 / +2 / +3) rings to grow your ball stream!
3. SQUARE & 45° TRIANGLE NEON BRICKS:
• Square & Triangle Bricks (`◢ ◣ ◤ ◥`): Bounce balls at 90° or 45° angles.
• Laser Orbs (`✚` `↔` `↕`): Zap entire rows and columns with neon beams when balls pass through.
• Bomb Bricks (`✹`): Explode violently and shatter surrounding 3×3 bricks.
• Armored, Rainbow, Gold & ×2 Bricks: Give massive bonus points and multipliers.
