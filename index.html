HOW TO PLAY

1. AIM & SHOOT BALLS: Drag finger or move mouse to aim the dual-bounce neon laser sight. Release or click to launch your volley of glowing balls!
2. ACTIVE LAUNCHER PADDLE: Move the glowing bottom paddle left/right while balls are airborne to bounce low balls back upward and collect falling Stars!
3. 8 NEON BRICK TYPES:
• Normal & Strong: Cyan/Pink glass bricks with numbered durability.
• Armored (Silver): Reinforced metallic frame with 3x durability.
• Explosive (Red ✹): Detonates on destruction, blasting 3x3 surrounding bricks.
• Laser (Cyan ✚): Fires vertical & horizontal neon laser beams when hit!
• Rainbow (HSL): Cycles neon colors and drops bonus multi-balls.
• Gold (★) & Multiplier (×2): Award bonus coins, stars, and boost score multiplier.
4. 10 TACTICAL POWER-UPS: Collect glowing power-up orbs on the board or tap any of the 10 bottom HUD buttons (using Stars ★ or keys 1–0) for Multi-Ball, Laser Attack, Lightning, Fireball, Explosion, Speed, Slow-Mo, +3 Balls, Shield, and Magnet!
